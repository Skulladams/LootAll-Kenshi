## BetterLooting

https://github.com/XxAtreuSSxX/BetterLooting — XxAtreuSSxX and contributors — GPLv3.

LootAll adapts the demonstrated active-inventory update approach, 10-units-per-meter spatial scan, dead/unconscious eligibility, engine section-placement checks, per-unit stack detach/add sequence, native theft provenance, destination fallback and rollback/invariant techniques. This is a separate project and does not redistribute or rename the BetterLooting DLL. Its authors' rights are retained. Reference commit and method names are recorded in `DEPENDENCIES.md` in the source archive.
